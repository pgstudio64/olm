Workstation
– Chair setback — Distance behind the desk to push back the chair
– Front desk access — Clear space in front of the desk to sit down
– Single desk access — Access space for an isolated desk (no facing neighbour)
– Desk to wall gap — Minimum distance between desk edge and wall
Circulation
– Passage behind row — Passage width behind a row — used to reach inner desks
– Back-to-back passage — Passage width between two back-to-back rows
– Circulation passage — Minimum passage width between blocks
– Main corridor width — Width of the main circulation corridor
Layout
– Min block separation — Minimum distance between two adjacent blocks
– Max island size — Maximum number of desks in a continuous island
– Door clearance zone — Depth of the clear zone in front of a door

Candidate solutions
Each room is matched against the pattern catalogue. Solutions are ranked by a weighted score combining several criteria.
Scoring weights
– Density — More desks in the room = higher score
– Comfort — Better circulation grade = higher score
– Back to door — Penalty for desks facing away from the door
– Natural light — Bonus for desks closer to windows
– Face to wall — Penalty for desks directly facing a blank wall
Circulation grades
– A — Min passage ≥ main corridor width
– B — Min passage > circulation passage
– C — Min passage = circulation passage
– D — Min passage ≥ 50% of circulation passage
– F — Min passage below 50% of circulation passage
Solution card
– Pattern name — Name and standard of the matched pattern
– Desks — Number of workstations placed in the room
– m²/desk — Room area divided by desk count
– Grade — Circulation grade (A to F)
– Score — Weighted total (higher = better)
